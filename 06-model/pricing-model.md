# The Model · FinWise

> Module 6 · Pricing & Monetization. The pricing & packaging recommendation memo for FinWise.

## Stage + model

**Stage:** Stage 1 · Value Creation, 1. Trial-to-Paid Conversion Rate: ~2% — Only 2 in every 100 trial users convert to paying customers, suggesting FinWise is struggling to demonstrate compelling value during the trial experience.

2. One-Year Paid Customer Retention: 40% — Approximately 60% of paying customers churn within their first year, suggesting that customers may not be experiencing sufficient ongoing value to sustain their subscriptions.

**Model:** Freemium, Encourages adoption and organic growth and allows users to experience value before paying. However conversion may remain low, and it could be difficult to define the free/paid boundary.

_____

## Recommendation

Move from a trial to paid conversion to a Freemium which would offer free tiering indefinitely with paid upgrades over time as users built habit and discover deeper functionality

**The pricing bet:** Changing pricing model, validating that users need longer to experience value before paying for upgrades.

_____

## Model signal

Financial modelling % usage.  This will measure whether users are progressing beyond initial setup to explore meaningful financial insights, providing an indicator of deeper product activation and value realisation.
