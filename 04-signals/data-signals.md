## Leading indicators

- **Activation Rate:** this measure should indicate that the users have completed key onboarding actions and they have experienced the products value and are activated.
- **Time to first key action:** the average time it takes a new user to complete a crucial in-product action immediately after sign-up to prove that there is fast access to value.
