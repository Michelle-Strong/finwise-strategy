## The data pattern

FinWise's 13 month data suggests a stronger relationship between frequency of engagement and paid conversion than between feature usage or session duration and conversion. However, the evidence is not statistically conclusive. 
Based on that information I would revise my hypothesis and opt for different leading measures.  
Firstly, I would opt for Time to First Key Action - the average time it takes a new user to complete a crucial in-product action immediately after sign-up to prove that there is fast access to value. And secondly, I would include Activation Rate -  this measure should indicate that the users have completed key onboarding actions and they have experienced the products value and are activated.

_Metric patterns: 
Conversion, CEILING - Conversion remains consistently around 2%, suggesting a persistent/ongoing barrier preventing further growth..
Modeling usage, CEILING - Appears to fluctuate between 10% & 50%, but doesn't breakthrough beyond that point.. 
Import usage, CEILING - remains broadly between 30% to 45% with some peaks and troughs but appears relatively consistent.. 
Session duration, SLOW LEAK - does appear to peak at 14 mins initially, but then decreases to 9 mins after 13 months.. 
Sessions/user, CLIFF - classic cliff pattern from the start of the data set, but then picks up again after 13 months.._

## Leading indicators

- **Financial Modelling % Usage:** Measures whether users are progressing beyond initial setup to explore meaningful financial insights, providing an indicator of deeper product activation and value realisation.
- **Get Started Import Feature Usage %:** Measures how effectively users complete the first step of importing their financial data, providing an early indicator of onboarding success.

_____

## Lagging indicators

- **Trial to Paid conversion rate:** This should demonstrate a customers willingness to pay and serve as a commercial bridge to the North Star
